Paradise Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Paradise Connect (the "App") is an internal business app owned by PT Super Kapita Propertindo, part of Progress Group ("we", "us", "our"). The App is used by our employees, mainly the sales team and their supervisors and managers, for attendance, managing prospect and customer data, unit reservations, and other sales activities. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how it is stored and protected.

The App is only available to Progress Group employees whose accounts have been registered by our HR department. It is not intended for the general public.

1. Information We Collect

Category Examples Purpose
Employee account information Name, email address, phone number, user ID, role, profile photo Sign-in, identity verification, account management, and role-based access to features
Precise location (GPS) Your coordinates while you use the attendance feature Verifying that you are within the allowed radius of the office, project site, or exhibition location when you clock in, clock out, or check in. Location is collected only while you use the attendance feature, not in the background.
Approximate location Location estimated from your IP address App usage analytics (see Google Firebase in Section 4)
Photos and files Attendance selfie photos; photos and documents attached to contacts, reservation orders, and booking payments; profile photo Proof of attendance and documentation of sales activities
Device contacts Name and phone number of a contact you select from your phone's address book Filling in the customer form faster. The App reads only the contact you select and does not upload your address book.
Customer and prospect data Names, phone/WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, addresses, identity card (KTP) and tax ID (NPWP) documents, reservation and payment details Managing prospects and customers and processing property unit reservations
WhatsApp conversations Conversation history between your linked WhatsApp number and prospects or customers Displaying conversations in the Inbox so the sales team and their supervisors can follow up on prospects. This applies only if you link your WhatsApp number to the Inbox feature.
Device information Device identifier, brand, model, device name, operating system version, app version, push notification token Sending push notifications, managing signed-in devices, and technical support
Usage data Features used, screens viewed, and in-app events, linked to your user ID and role Understanding how the App is used and improving it

Face ID / Touch ID: if you enable biometric sign-in, verification is performed entirely on your device by iOS. The App never receives or stores your biometric data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing the App's features: attendance verification, prospect and customer management, unit reservations, sales kit, tasks, and notifications.
  • Team management: supervisors and managers can view the attendance and sales activity of the sales staff in their team, according to the sales hierarchy.
  • Security: verifying identity and preventing misuse, such as attendance recorded outside the allowed location.
  • Analytics: understanding usage patterns to fix problems and improve the App.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not sell your data.

3. Device Permissions

The App asks for permissions only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Camera: taking attendance photos and photographing documents.
  • Location: verifying your location during attendance.
  • Photos: choosing photos or files to attach.
  • Contacts: importing a contact into the customer form.
  • Notifications: receiving task, approval, and reservation status updates.
  • Face ID: optional quick sign-in.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device's Settings. The rest of the App keeps working, but the feature that needs the permission will not be available.

4. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell user data. Data is shared only as follows:

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Cloud Messaging): receives usage data, approximate location, device identifiers, and push notification tokens for analytics and notification delivery. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp: if you link your WhatsApp number to the Inbox, our servers retrieve the conversation history of that number from WhatsApp in order to display it in the App.
  • Embedded content: some sales kit materials include embedded YouTube videos. When you play them, YouTube (Google) may collect data according to its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose data if required by law or by a valid request from authorities.

All other operational data (attendance, location, photos, files, prospect, customer, and reservation data) is stored on servers managed by Progress Group.

5. Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to data is restricted by role, so each user can only see the data they need for their job.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep data for as long as your account is active and as needed for the company's operational and legal record-keeping. When your employment ends, your account is deactivated. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below. We will process requests in accordance with Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection and our internal policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended only for our employees and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
